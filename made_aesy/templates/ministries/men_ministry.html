KAMA — Kenya Anglican Men’s Association

Building responsible Christian men grounded in faith, service and leadership.

Our Mission

To empower men spiritually, socially and economically to become pillars of their families, the Church and the community, according to the ACK KAMA mandate.

Programs & Activities

⛪ Men’s Fellowship

Weekly sessions for Bible study, discipleship and Christian mentorship.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Strengthening

Training on fatherhood, marriage enrichment and family stewardship.

🛠 Community Service

Church maintenance, missions, charity work and community support programs.

💼 Economic Empowerment

Business forums, investment groups, skills development and savings programs.

🎤 Men’s Choir

Active participation in church worship through singing and liturgy support.
