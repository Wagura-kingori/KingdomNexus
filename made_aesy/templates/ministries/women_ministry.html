Women’s Guild (Mothers’ Union)

Empowering women spiritually, socially, and in Christian service.

Our Mission

To nurture Christian families, empower women, support church programs, and uphold moral, spiritual, and social growth in society according to the ACK Mothers’ Union values.

Core Activities

✝️ Bible Study & Fellowship

Weekly fellowship focused on spiritual growth, scripture study and discipleship.

❤️ Marriage & Family Support

Mentorship for young mothers, marriage guidance, home visits and prayer support.

🤝 Charity & Outreach

Hospital visits, supporting the needy, community projects and social welfare missions.

🪡 Skills & Economic Empowerment

Training in crafts, financial stewardship, savings groups, and leadership development.

🎶 Choir & Worship Support

Women’s Guild choir leads praise, worship and supports church liturgical activities.
