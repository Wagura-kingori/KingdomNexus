Children Ministry

Building a Christ-centered foundation for our children through love, learning and worship.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To nurture children in the knowledge and fear of God, helping them grow spiritually, morally and emotionally through biblical teaching and mentorship.

Vision

To raise a generation of God-fearing boys and girls who will serve Christ faithfully in church and society.

Age Groups

• Beginners (3–5 yrs)

Interactive Bible stories, songs, memory verses and fun learning.

• Middle Class (6–8 yrs)

Foundational Bible understanding, worship, teamwork and crafts.

• Pre-Teens (9–12 yrs)

Deeper Bible study, leadership skills, Christian values and talent development.

Weekly Programs

Sunday School — Every Sunday
  • Bible lessons with visual aids
  • Children praise & worship
  • Memory verse challenge
  • Creative crafts and games
  • Prayer and life-application discussions
Saturday Fellowship (10:00AM – 12:30PM)
  • Bible study classes
  • Music & drama practice
  • Leadership & discipline training
  • Skill workshops (art, dance, music)

Bible Curriculum

ACK Children Ministry follows a structured curriculum covering:

  • Creation, Patriarchs & Early Bible characters
  • Life and teachings of Jesus Christ
  • Parables and miracles
  • Christian values — honesty, kindness, respect
  • Sacramental teaching in a child-friendly format
  • Church seasons — Advent, Lent, Easter, Christmas

Clubs & Activities

• Music, Dance & Drama (MDD)

Choir, memory verses, skits and worship expressions.

• Junior Readers & Bible Quiz Club

Children learn to interpret scriptures and compete in quiz challenges.

• Talent Development

Drawing, dancing, singing, storytelling and more.

Events & Retreats

  • Annual VBS (Vacation Bible School)
  • Children Sunday & Presentations
  • Weekend retreats & prayer camps
  • Talent exhibitions
  • Mission & charity visits

Child Safety & Protection

ACK Church prioritizes child safety. All teachers and volunteers:

  • Are vetted and trained on child protection
  • Follow church safeguarding policies
  • Ensure a safe, respectful learning environment
  • Maintain proper supervision during all activities
